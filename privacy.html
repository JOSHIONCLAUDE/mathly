Mathly — Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 2026

Mathly is a free maths calculator and problem solver. We collect nothing about you. There are no accounts, no ads and no trackers, and the app works fully offline.

What stays on your device

Your calculation history and settings (theme, degrees or radians, fraction mode) are saved only on your own device. Everything is calculated on your device and we never receive any of it. Clearing the app data deletes it.

What we do not do

  • We do not collect, store, sell or share personal information.
  • We do not use your location, contacts, camera or microphone.
  • We do not show advertising.

Children

The app is safe for children because it collects no personal information at all.
